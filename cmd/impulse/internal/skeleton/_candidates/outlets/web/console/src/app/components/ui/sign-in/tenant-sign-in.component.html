Who may enter {{ tenantName() || 'this tenant' }}

A tenant's directory is the organization its people sign in through, outside this application: a Google Workspace or a Microsoft Entra tenant. A tenant has one directory, and a directory belongs to one tenant. Nobody types a directory in: an administrator of this tenant who is signed in through the organization's directory makes it this tenant's, here. A person who signs in through the directory is given the default role at their first sign-in, so they enter at once; with no default role they are told this tenant admits people by invitation only, and an invited person enters with the roles the invitation names. Passwords are this application's own way in, and the tenant admits them unless it is directory only: then its people enter through its directory alone, and a password does not reach it. A person whose every tenant is directory only cannot sign in with a password at all.
