Who may enter {{ tenantName() || 'this tenant' }}

A tenant's directory is the organization its people sign in through, outside this application: a Google Workspace or a Microsoft Entra tenant. A tenant has one directory, and a directory belongs to one tenant. Nobody types a directory in: an administrator of this tenant who is signed in through the organization's directory makes it this tenant's, here. A tenant that is open to its directory's people gives each of them a role at their first sign-in through the directory, so they enter at once; a tenant that is invite only tells them it admits people by invitation, and an invited person enters with the roles the invitation names.

A person seated in this tenant enters it however they signed in: with a password, with a personal account, or through another organization's directory. A tenant that is directory only admits only the people who sign in through its own directory, and keeps everyone else seated in it out for as long as that is on. The setting closes this tenant alone: a person it keeps out still enters every other tenant that seats them, and a person kept out of every tenant that seats them is refused at sign-in.
