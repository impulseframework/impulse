Who may enter {{ sectorName() || 'this sector' }}

A sector's directory is the organization its people sign in through, outside this application: a Google Workspace or a Microsoft Entra tenant. A sector has one directory, and a directory belongs to one sector. Nobody types a directory in: a marshal of this sector who is signed in through the organization's directory makes it this sector's, here. A sector that is open to its directory's people gives each of them a role at their first sign-in through the directory, so they enter at once; a sector that is invite only tells them it admits people by invitation, and an invited person enters with the roles the invitation names.

A person seated in this sector enters it however they signed in: with a password, with a personal account, or through another organization's directory. A sector that is directory only admits only the people who sign in through its own directory, and keeps everyone else seated in it out for as long as that is on. The setting closes this sector alone: a person it keeps out still enters every other sector that seats them, and a person kept out of every sector that seats them is refused at sign-in.
