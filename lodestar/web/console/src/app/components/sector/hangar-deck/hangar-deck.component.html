Drag a ship onto the next bay to fire the transition — a bay accepts the drop only when the ship's own capability envelope lists that edge for you; three bays can send a ship to the scrapyard. The deck is empty outside your shift: the Dockmaster holds it 06:00–18:00 headquarters time, the Night Watch the rest.
